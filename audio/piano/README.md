# Piano samples

Salamander Grand Piano V3 by Alexander Holm, a Yamaha C5 recorded one note every minor third
from A0 to C8. Licensed under [CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).
Source: https://archive.org/details/SalamanderGrandPianoV3, in the MP3 cut published by
Tone.js (https://github.com/Tonejs/audio, `salamander/`).

Changed here: notes longer than nine seconds are cut at nine seconds (a stream copy, not a
re-encode). The app fades each one out before its end, so the cut is never heard.

`A0 C1 Ds1 Fs1 … C8` are the file names; `Ds` is D sharp and `Fs` is F sharp. The app pitches
each sample at most a semitone up or down to reach the notes between them.
