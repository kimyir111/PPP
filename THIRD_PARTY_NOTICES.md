## YouTube links

When you paste a YouTube link, PPP fetches that video's audio (on the server, or on your own PC when you use the PC worker) only to write the notes it hears; the audio is not kept on the server. Use recordings you have the right to use. The score PPP writes is for your own practice: a score made from a YouTube link cannot be posted to Shared Scores, although you can still send its link.
