## YouTube links

When you paste a YouTube link, PPP fetches that video's audio (on the server, or on your own PC when you use the PC worker) only to write the notes it hears; the audio is not kept on the server. If YouTube refuses the server, the server falls back to a third-party download site (loader.to), which then receives the link you pasted. Your browser also contacts YouTube to show the video (the youtube-nocookie.com player) and, when PPP's server cannot, to look up its title (oEmbed). Use recordings you have the right to use. The score PPP writes is for your own practice: a score made from a YouTube link cannot be posted to Shared Scores, although you can still send its link.
